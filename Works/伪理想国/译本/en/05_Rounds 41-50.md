Scheien: The more finely the six are written, the more they prove the representative must be a human.

Cecil: The machine remains the table that is represented, counted and pointed at. Article 24 of the Personal Information Protection Law requires automated decision-making to be transparent and fair, forbids unreasonable differential treatment, allows a decision with major impact to be explained on demand, and allows refusal of decisions made solely by automated means. It writes contestability as a legal right: the person affected must be able to seek an account from someone who can answer back, not from a piece of output.

Scheien: You will use Arendt and Jonas together.

Cecil: Arendt's The Human Condition ties action to natality and unpredictability in the human; Jonas's The Imperative of Responsibility says the technological age needs an ethics of responsibility directed at long-term consequences, and a "heuristics of fear." The further the consequence, the more there must be a human who can answer in advance; responsibility cannot be spread onto a program that knows no fear.

Scheien: And the case law points the same way.

Cecil: On 14 February 2024 the Civil Resolution Tribunal of British Columbia decided Moffatt v. Air Canada: the chatbot misstated the bereavement fare, the airline's defence that "the chatbot is an independent legal entity" was rejected, and it was ordered to perform and to pay. The court did not investigate whether the robot had reason; it asked who must answer and who must pay, and the answer fell on the company.

Scheien: So you say existing law already suffices.

Cecil: Product liability, insurance, human oversight and the right to explanation under Article 24 catch all four questions, attribution, imputation, rights, institution, and no box need be added. Who pays, who explains, who is punished, all three stay with human beings, not one is moved outward. As this group closes, I leave the sentence as a concrete demand you can take up: beyond contract, insurance, product liability and human oversight, which right or duty is it that cannot be realized unless a registrable, windable "subject" is written onto the list? And say, by the way: when that subject is shut down, who explains to whom; and after winding-up, to which concrete human being the residual property goes.
